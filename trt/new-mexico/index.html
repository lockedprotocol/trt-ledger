Common questions

How much does TRT cost in New Mexico?

Online clinics that confirm they serve New Mexico start at about $1,548 for the first year (PeterMD), including startup fees. Insurance may cover part of the cost when low testosterone is diagnosed; cash-pay clinics usually don't bill insurance.

Can I get TRT online in New Mexico?

Yes. PeterMD and 1 other online clinic confirm they treat patients in New Mexico.

Do I need blood work before starting TRT?

Clinics generally require recent blood tests showing low testosterone before they prescribe. You can order your own lab tests without a doctor visit in most states.
