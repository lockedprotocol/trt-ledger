Common questions

How much does TRT cost in Arkansas?

Online clinics that confirm they serve Arkansas start at about $1,548 for the first year (PeterMD), including startup fees. In person, Low T Center lists $25 first test · $135 confirmation + first shot · ~$105.50/mo injections · $135 follow-ups. Insurance may cover part of the cost when low testosterone is diagnosed; cash-pay clinics usually don't bill insurance.

Can I get TRT online in Arkansas?

Yes. PeterMD and 1 other online clinic confirm they treat patients in Arkansas.

Do I need blood work before starting TRT?

Clinics generally require recent blood tests showing low testosterone before they prescribe. You can order your own lab tests without a doctor visit in most states.
