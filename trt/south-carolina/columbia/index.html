Common questions

How much does TRT cost in Columbia?

Online clinics that confirm they serve South Carolina start at about $1,548 for the first year (PeterMD), including startup fees. Insurance may cover part of the cost when low testosterone is diagnosed; cash-pay clinics usually don't bill insurance.

Where can I get TRT in Columbia, SC?

Do I need blood work before starting TRT?

Clinics generally require recent blood tests showing low testosterone before they prescribe. You can order your own lab tests without a doctor visit in most states.
