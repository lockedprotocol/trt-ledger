Common questions

How much does TRT cost in Georgia?

Online clinics that confirm they serve Georgia start at about $1,548 for the first year (PeterMD), including startup fees. Insurance may cover part of the cost when low testosterone is diagnosed; cash-pay clinics usually don't bill insurance.

Can I get TRT online in Georgia?

Yes. PeterMD and 3 other online clinics confirm they treat patients in Georgia.

Do I need blood work before starting TRT?

Clinics generally require recent blood tests showing low testosterone before they prescribe. You can order your own lab tests without a doctor visit in most states.
