Common questions

How much does TRT cost in Maryland?

Online clinics that confirm they serve Maryland start at about $1,548 for the first year (PeterMD), including startup fees. Insurance may cover part of the cost when low testosterone is diagnosed; cash-pay clinics usually don't bill insurance.

Can I get TRT online in Maryland?

Yes. PeterMD and 2 other online clinics confirm they treat patients in Maryland.

Do I need blood work before starting TRT?

Clinics generally require recent blood tests showing low testosterone before they prescribe. You can order your own lab tests without a doctor visit in most states.
