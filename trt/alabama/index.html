Common questions

How much does TRT cost in Alabama?

Online clinics that confirm they serve Alabama start at about $2,988 for the first year (Fountain TRT), including startup fees. Insurance may cover part of the cost when low testosterone is diagnosed; cash-pay clinics usually don't bill insurance.

Can I get TRT online in Alabama?

Yes. Fountain TRT confirms it treats patients in Alabama.

Do I need blood work before starting TRT?

Clinics generally require recent blood tests showing low testosterone before they prescribe. You can order your own lab tests without a doctor visit in most states.
