Common questions

How much does TRT cost in Renton?

Online clinics that confirm they serve Washington start at about $1,548 for the first year (PeterMD), including startup fees. Insurance may cover part of the cost when low testosterone is diagnosed; cash-pay clinics usually don't bill insurance.

Where can I get TRT in Renton, WA?

7 clinic listings in Renton: 1 TRT or men's health clinic, 3 urology practices and 3 endocrinology practices. Urologists and endocrinologists can prescribe testosterone, but not all of them offer TRT — call and ask.

Can I get TRT online in Washington?

Yes. PeterMD and 4 other online clinics confirm they treat patients in Washington.

Do I need blood work before starting TRT?

Clinics generally require recent blood tests showing low testosterone before they prescribe. You can order your own lab tests without a doctor visit in most states.
